About Biofy

Built in a research lab.Hardened for production.

Biofy is a Norwegian deep-tech company commercialising cryptobiometric identity, developed over a decade at the Norwegian Information Security Laboratory (NISlab) at NTNU.

01

Mission

Make biometric identity safe enough to deploy anywhere — without demanding that users trust the operator of the database. We treat identity data the way cryptographers treat secrets: protected in storage, protected in transit, revocable on compromise.

02

Origin

The biometric cryptosystem was developed at NISlab (NTNU) across three EU-funded research projects — TURBINE, FIDELITY, and PIDaaS — producing the foundations of a private identity platform piloted in e-Citizen, e-Commerce, and e-Health scenarios.

03

Today

We operate in healthcare, corporate eID, and home care across the Nordics, partnering with public-sector institutions, national trust services, and telehealth providers. The Biofy platform is now available for self-service pilots and API integration.

Principles
◇

Irreversible by construction

If our database leaks, no biometric is recoverable from it. Not a promise — a property of the math.

◇

Revocable

Credentials can be rotated per user, per tenant, per breach. Biology doesn't have to be.

◇

Interoperable

We speak FIDO2, OIDC, SAML, and the eIDAS trust framework. We don't ask customers to leave their stack.

◇

Auditable

Open academic record. Patents where protection is warranted. No security through obscurity.

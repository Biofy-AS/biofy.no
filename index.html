How it works

A credential, not a copy.

Traditional biometric systems store a reference copy of your fingerprint or face. If that database is breached, the damage is permanent — you cannot issue a new fingerprint. Biofy works differently: a biometric is used to generate a revocable credential. What is stored cannot be worked backwards to reconstruct the person. If it is ever compromised, the credential is cancelled and reissued. Available as a managed API — no cryptography expertise required.

01

Capture

Modality-agnostic sensor input — face, fingerprint, iris, voice.

02

Derive

The platform generates a stable, unique credential from the biometric — without storing a copy of it.

03

Bind

The credential is bound to application context — tenant, role, session, policy.

04

Verify

Subsequent captures reproduce the credential on-device. No raw biometric leaves the endpoint.
